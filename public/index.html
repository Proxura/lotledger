Which batch did that jar come from? Answer in five seconds, not an afternoon.

LotLedger is the batch record for people who make things in a kitchen, a mill room or a garage and sell them at markets and online. Every lot, every best-by, every unit out, in one place you can open on your phone.

52 seconds, with sound. Log a batch, record units out, scan a label, export the Pirate Ship file.

Log a batch in 30 seconds

Product, date, how many, what went in and who supplied it. The lot number assigns itself. Best-by fills in from the product's shelf life.

Know what to sell first

Sell-soon and past-best-by alerts on every lot. Units on hand by product, always current, because every sale, sample and shrink is a tap.

Recall-ready records

Put a lot on hold with a reason. Export the whole ledger, every lot and every movement, as one CSV when an inspector or a customer asks.

Scan a label, open the lot

Print QR labels for any lot from inside the app, 30 to a sheet. Point your phone at one and the lot opens, ready to record units out. No scanner hardware, no app store.

Wired to your shop

Paste one link into Stripe or Shopify and paid orders land in LotLedger by themselves, already packed from the oldest lot. Your website reads which products are sold out. The QR on a jar opens that batch for your customer: when it was made, what went in, who made it.

Ship with one click

Import your Shopify, Etsy, Square or Stripe orders, set weights, and download the exact spreadsheet Pirate Ship's upload accepts. Exported orders are marked so nothing ships twice.

Your data is yours

JSON backup any time. Import it back, or import the records from a spreadsheet you kept before.

Support from the maker

Email from inside the app and you get an answer from the person who built it, usually the same day.
